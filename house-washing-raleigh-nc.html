What Is House Washing and Why Does It Matter?

Your home's exterior is constantly exposed to algae, mold, mildew, pollen, bird droppings, spider webs, and general grime. Over time, these elements don't just make your home look dirty — they can actually damage your siding, cause paint to peel, and create conditions for moisture intrusion.

Professional house washing by Halo Pressure Washing removes all of that safely and thoroughly. We use a low-pressure soft wash system with biodegradable, eco-friendly cleaning solutions that eliminate organic growth at the root — not just scrub the surface clean. The result lasts significantly longer than a standard water-only rinse.

Carter and the Halo team are trained in the right techniques for each surface type. We never use the same high-pressure settings on vinyl siding that we'd use on a concrete driveway. Every surface gets the treatment it needs — and nothing more.

Surface Types We Clean

Different surfaces require different approaches. We know the difference.

Vinyl Siding

Vinyl is the most common siding material in Raleigh's neighborhoods, and it's particularly prone to algae and mildew growth due to its porous texture. Our soft wash treatment safely removes green and black organic growth from vinyl without warping, cracking, or blowing water behind panels. We clean the entire exterior including soffits, fascia, and trim.

Brick Exteriors

Brick is durable but porous — meaning mold, mildew, and efflorescence (white mineral deposits) can work their way into the surface. We use low-pressure washing with appropriate cleaning agents to restore brick to its original color and texture. Whether it's a full brick home or brick accents, we treat it with the care this classic material deserves.

Stucco

Stucco requires especially careful handling — high pressure can crack or gouge the surface, leading to water intrusion and costly repairs. Our soft wash approach uses gentle pressure and professional cleaning solutions to safely remove algae, mold, and staining from stucco exteriors. We protect your investment while delivering excellent results.

Wood Siding

Wood siding is beautiful but susceptible to mold, mildew, and weathering. Too much pressure can raise the grain or damage the finish. We calibrate our equipment carefully for wood surfaces, removing biological growth and grime while preserving the integrity of the wood. This is also ideal prep work before repainting or staining your exterior.
