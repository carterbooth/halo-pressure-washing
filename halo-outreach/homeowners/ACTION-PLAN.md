## Week 4: Referral Engine (April 14-20)

### By now you should have:
- 5-10 completed jobs from homeowners
- Some happy customers

### New focus: Turn customers into referral sources

**After every job:**
1. Text customer: "Thanks for trusting us! Mind leaving a review on our Facebook page? [link]"
2. Offer neighbor discount: "If any of your neighbors book in the next 2 weeks, you both get $25 off. Just have them mention your name!"

**Post customer transformations:**
- "Shoutout to the [Last Name] family in Holly Springs! Check out their driveway transformation. [photos]"
- Tag customer (if they're comfortable)
- Generates social proof + word-of-mouth

**Track referrals:**
- "How did you hear about us?" → Every new lead
- Identify top referral sources (neighbors, Facebook, NextDoor)

## Week 5-8: Sustain & Scale (Rest of April/May)

### Maintenance mode (1 hour/week):
- 2-3 NextDoor posts per week
- 3-5 Facebook business page posts per week
- Respond to ALL recommendation requests within 2 hours
- 30 door hangers per week in new neighborhood

### Scale tactics:
1. **Ask for video testimonials**
   - "Quick favor - would you record a 15-second video saying why you're happy with the results?"
   - Post to Facebook/NextDoor (video > photos for engagement)

2. **Neighborhood takeover strategy**
   - When you book 1 job in a neighborhood, offer neighbor discount to entire street
   - "Doing 3 driveways on Oak Ridge Lane this Saturday. Text for $25 off if you're on the same street!"
   - Batch jobs = more efficient + creates FOMO

3. **Partner with complementary businesses**
   - Landscapers (they see dirty driveways all the time)
   - Real estate photographers (pre-listing curb appeal)
   - Window cleaners (cross-promote)
   - Offer 10% referral commission

## Red Flags (When to Pivot)

**If after 2 weeks:**
- NextDoor posts get 0-1 comments → Try different content angle
- Facebook groups delete your posts → You're being too salesy, dial it back
- Door hangers get 0 responses → Wrong neighborhoods or bad design

**If after 4 weeks:**
- Still <2 homeowner leads per week → Focus on B2B (property managers, agents, HOAs) instead
- Spending more time on marketing than jobs → Scale back, focus on referrals

---

## When You've "Made It" (Goal State)

**You know you've nailed homeowner marketing when:**
- 5+ leads per week WITHOUT active posting (word-of-mouth is working)
- Neighbors tag you in Facebook/NextDoor posts
- You're booked 10-14 days out
- 30%+ of new leads are referrals

**At that point:**
- Stop door hangers (time > return)
- Reduce social posting to 1-2x per week (maintenance mode)
- Focus on delivering great work (referrals sustain you)
