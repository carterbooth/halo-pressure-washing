# Facebook Neighborhood Groups Strategy

## Why Facebook Groups Work for Homeowners

- Neighborhood-specific (Holly Springs Neighbors, Apex Community, etc.)
- High engagement (people LIVE in these groups)
- Visual platform (before/after photos pop)
- Easy sharing (word-of-mouth spreads fast)
- Free marketing (no ads needed)

## Setup (20 minutes)

### 1. Find & Join Groups

Search Facebook for:
- "[City Name] Neighbors"
- "[City Name] Community Group"
- "[Neighborhood Name] HOA" (if public)
- "Wake County Home Services"
- "Raleigh Area Homeowners"

**Target groups:**
- Holly Springs Neighbors & Community Groups
- Fuquay Varina Community
- Apex Neighbors
- Cary Community Groups
- North Raleigh Neighbors
- Garner Area Groups

**Join 10-15 active groups** (answer their screening questions honestly)

### 2. Profile Optimization

- **Profile photo:** Professional headshot or you with equipment
- **Cover photo:** Halo Pressure Washing logo or before/after transformation
- **Intro:** "Local pressure washing in Wake County | Co-owner of Halo Pressure Washing | Fuquay Varina"
- **Add to Featured:** Link to business page or contact info

### 3. Create Halo Business Page

- **Page name:** Halo Pressure Washing
- **Category:** Cleaning Service / Home Improvement
- **About:** "Professional pressure washing for driveways, patios, and home exteriors. Serving Raleigh, Holly Springs, Fuquay Varina, Apex, Cary, and surrounding areas. Family-owned, local, reliable."
- **Contact:** (910) 538-6552
- **Photos:** Before/after gallery, you working, equipment

## Posting Strategy

### Rule #1: Groups Hate Self-Promotion
Most neighborhood groups ban blatant advertising. Instead:
- **Answer questions** ("Looking for pressure washing recs?")
- **Provide value** (seasonal tips, DIY advice)
- **Share transformations** (framed as community pride, not ads)
- **Engage authentically** (comment on non-business posts too)

### Posting Frequency

**In groups:** 1-2x per week MAX per group  
**On your business page:** 3-5x per week

## Groups to AVOID

❌ Strictly "no self-promotion" groups (you'll get banned)  
❌ Groups with <100 active members (not worth the effort)  
❌ Groups outside your 30-mile service radius  
❌ Groups that are just spam/ads (no real community)
