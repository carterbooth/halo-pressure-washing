# Door Hanger Strategy - What Worked, What Didn't

## What Happened

**The Results:**
- 80 door hangers distributed
- 1 call received
- 1 estimate given ($575)
- Customer went with lower bid
- **Conversion rate: 0%**

## Why It Didn't Work (Analysis)

### Problem 1: Price Shoppers
Door hangers attract price-sensitive customers in mid-tier neighborhoods. They're comparing 5+ quotes and going with the cheapest.

### Problem 2: No Differentiation
If your door hanger just says "pressure washing" with a phone number, you're competing purely on price. You need a reason they should call YOU, not just "a pressure washer."

### Problem 3: Wrong Neighborhoods
Mid-tier neighborhoods = tighter budgets = price shopping. You need to target differently.

---

## How to Fix It (Keep Door Hangers, Optimize Approach)

### Strategy 1: Target High-Value Neighborhoods

**Look for:**
- Houses $400K+ (Zillow/Realtor filters)
- Well-maintained landscaping (people who care about curb appeal)
- Visibly dirty driveways (your service solves an obvious problem)
- HOA communities (neighbors compete on appearance)

**Avoid:**
- Rental-heavy areas
- Fixer-uppers
- Neighborhoods where most driveways are already clean

**Best neighborhoods in your area:**
- North Raleigh (near Falls Lake)
- West Cary (Preston, Lochmere)
- Apex (Bella Casa, Salem Village)
- Holly Springs (newer developments off 55)

### Strategy 3: Timing & Targeting

**When to distribute:**
- **After heavy pollen days** (people are annoyed, ready to act)
- **After rain** (driveways look EXTRA gross when wet)
- **Weekends** (people are home, see the hanger immediately)
- **Early spring** (March-April = peak pollen awareness)

**How to target:**
1. **Drive the neighborhood first** - Only hit houses with visibly dirty driveways
2. **Look for "cues"** - Well-maintained landscaping + dirty driveway = they care about curb appeal
3. **Avoid:** Already-clean driveways (they either just had it done or DIY it themselves)

**Distribution strategy:**
- 20-30 hangers per neighborhood (focused, not scattered)
- Hit the same neighborhood 2-3 times over a month (repetition works)
- Leave hangers on weekends when people are home

---

### Strategy 4: The Follow-Up System

Most people throw away the first door hanger. Second or third time? They pay attention.

**Week 1:** Distribute 30 hangers in Neighborhood A  
**Week 2:** Distribute 30 hangers in Neighborhood B  
**Week 3:** Redistribute 30 hangers in Neighborhood A (different streets)  
**Week 4:** Redistribute 30 hangers in Neighborhood B  

**You're creating frequency** - they see your name 2-3 times = brand recognition.

---

### Strategy 5: Add Urgency & Scarcity

**On the door hanger:**

"🚨 SPRING POLLEN SEASON - LIMITED AVAILABILITY"

"We're booking 7-10 days out. Text this week to secure your spot before May."

**Why it works:**
- Creates urgency (don't procrastinate)
- Signals demand (other people want you = social proof)
- Filters serious buyers (price shoppers won't care about availability)

---

### Strategy 6: The "Neighbor Discount" Hook

**On door hanger:**

"✨ NEIGHBOR DISCOUNT ACTIVE ✨

We're doing 3+ driveways on your street this week.
Text to join the batch - save $25 when your neighbors book too."

**Why it works:**
- Social proof (your neighbors trust us)
- FOMO (don't want to be the only dirty driveway on the block)
- Group discount = feels like a deal without lowering your base price

---

### Strategy 7: Make It EASY to Respond

**Don't make them call.** Calling = friction. Texting = instant.

**Door hanger CTA:**

"📲 TEXT a photo of your driveway to (910) 538-6552
We'll send your quote in under 10 minutes."

**Include a QR code** that opens a pre-filled text:
"Hi! Saw your door hanger. I'm interested in a driveway cleaning quote. My address is [their address auto-filled if possible]."

## Math: Old vs. New Approach

### Old Approach (What Happened):
- 80 hangers → 1 call → 0 bookings = **0% conversion**
- Cost: $20 (printing) + 2 hours (distribution) = **ROI: -100%**

### New Approach (Optimized):
- 30 hangers (targeted, high-value neighborhood)
- 3-5 responses (visibly dirty driveways + better design)
- 2 bookings @ $200-250 each = **$400-500 revenue**
- Cost: $10 (printing) + 1 hour (distribution) = **ROI: +3000-4000%**

**Key difference:** Fewer hangers, better targeting, stronger messaging.

## New Door Hanger Workflow

1. **Scout neighborhood** (drive through, identify dirty driveways)
2. **Print 30 targeted hangers** (Vistaprint, $0.15-0.30 each)
3. **Distribute Saturday morning** (people see it immediately)
4. **Follow up Monday** (text everyone who responded)
5. **Repeat same neighborhood 2 weeks later** (build frequency)

---

## When to Stop Using Door Hangers

Once you have:
- 5+ jobs per week booked from other channels (NextDoor, Facebook, B2B emails)
- More demand than you can handle
- Higher-value clients (real estate agents, HOAs, property managers)

**At that point:** Door hangers are too time-consuming for the return. Focus on scalable marketing (social media, email, referrals).

## Print Resources

- Canva (free design templates)
- GotPrint (bulk discounts)

**Professional design:**
- Fiverr ($20-50 for custom design)
- 99designs (if you want multiple options to choose from)

**DIY design tips:**
- Use actual before/after photos (not stock images)
- Keep text LARGE (people read door hangers from 3 feet away)
- High contrast (dark text on light background)
- QR code should be at least 1.5" x 1.5"

## Bottom Line

Door hangers CAN work, but only if:
1. You target the RIGHT neighborhoods (high-value, dirty driveways)
2. Your design stands out (before/after photos, urgency, social proof)
3. You make it EASY to respond (text, not call)
4. You create frequency (hit same area 2-3 times)

**Don't give up on door hangers yet.** You just need to optimize the approach.

But ALSO double down on NextDoor + Facebook (higher ROI, less legwork).
