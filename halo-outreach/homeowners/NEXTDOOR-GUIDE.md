# NextDoor Strategy for Halo Pressure Washing

## Why NextDoor is Gold for Driveways

- Hyper-local (your exact service area)
- High-intent (people actively looking for recommendations)
- Trust-based (neighbors vouching for you)
- Free organic reach
- Homeowners with disposable income

1. **Create account:** nextdoor.com

3. **Join nearby neighborhoods:**
   - Holly Springs

4. **Complete profile:**
   - Photo: Professional headshot or you with equipment
   - Bio: "Co-owner of Halo Pressure Washing. Helping neighbors restore their homes' curb appeal since [year]. Based in Fuquay Varina."
   - Business page: Create "Halo Pressure Washing" listing

## Posting Strategy

### Rule #1: Don't Be Salesy
NextDoor HATES hard sells. You'll get reported and banned. Instead:
- **Give value first** (tips, seasonal advice)
- **Show transformations** (before/after photos)
- **Answer questions** (become the local expert)
- **Light CTA** (mention availability, not prices)

### Posting Schedule (2-3x per week)

**Monday/Wednesday/Friday mornings (7-9 AM)**  
→ Homeowners scrolling before work

**Topics to rotate:**

1. **Educational posts** (builds authority)
2. **Before/After transformations** (visual proof)
3. **Seasonal reminders** (creates urgency)
4. **Neighbor shoutouts** (social proof)
5. **Special offers** (occasional, not every post)
