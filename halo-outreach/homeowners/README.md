# Homeowner Lead Generation Strategy

**Goal:** Get driveway jobs from direct homeowners (not just B2B)

---

## Why Homeowners Are Different

You can't scrape residential emails like you can business contacts. Cold emails to homeowners = spam complaints = killed deliverability.

Instead, use **community-based, high-trust channels:**

---

## The 3 Channels

### 1. NextDoor (HIGHEST ROI)
**Why it works:** People actively ask for recommendations. You're answering, not cold pitching.

**Time investment:** 30 min/day  
**Expected return:** 3-5 leads/week once established  

**Read:** `NEXTDOOR-GUIDE.md` + `nextdoor-posts.md`

---

### 2. Facebook Neighborhood Groups
**Why it works:** Visual platform (before/after photos pop) + massive reach.

**Time investment:** 30 min/day  
**Expected return:** 2-3 leads/week once established  

**Read:** `FACEBOOK-STRATEGY.md`

---

### 3. Optimized Door Hangers
**Why it works:** When done RIGHT (targeted neighborhoods, better design, urgency/scarcity), they convert.

**Time investment:** 1 hour/week  
**Expected return:** 1-2 leads/week if targeted well  

**Read:** `DOOR-HANGER-OPTIMIZATION.md`

---
