**Subject:** "PSA: Pine Pollen is Eating Your Driveway"

Hey neighbors! 👋

Quick heads up for this time of year - that yellow film covering everything isn't just annoying, it's actually acidic and can stain concrete driveways if left too long (especially in our NC humidity).

**Quick test:** Run your finger across your driveway. If it leaves a trail, you've got buildup that needs removal before it sets in.

**DIY tip:** Regular garden hose won't cut it - you need pressure to remove the embedded pollen. If you don't have a pressure washer, most hardware stores rent them for $50-80/day.

We're seeing a ton of pollen this spring in Fuquay/Holly Springs. Stay on top of it before it becomes a permanent stain!

## POST 5: DIY vs Pro Comparison (Educational + Soft Sell)

**Subject:** "Should You DIY Your Driveway? Honest Breakdown"

Saw a few posts asking about DIY pressure washing, so here's the real deal:

**When DIY makes sense:**
- Small patio or deck (&lt;200 sq ft)
- You already own a pressure washer
- You've got a free Saturday and don't mind the workout

**When to call a pro:**
- Full driveway (most are 500-1000 sq ft)
- Two-story house exterior
- You value your weekend time
- You want it done RIGHT (wrong pressure = damage)

**Cost comparison:**
- Rental: $60-80/day + your time (4-6 hours for a driveway)
- Pro job: $150-250 depending on size, done in 1-2 hours

I'm biased obviously (this is my business), but I've seen too many folks rent equipment, spend their whole Saturday on it, and still have streaks/missed spots.

Not trying to upsell - just want neighbors to make an informed choice. If you DO go DIY, keep the nozzle moving and use a fan tip (not the red zero-degree tip unless you want grooves in your concrete).

Questions? Drop 'em below. Always happy to help, even if you're going the DIY route.

## POST 7: Educational Tip (Value-First, No Sell)

**Subject:** "3 Signs Your Driveway Needs More Than Cleaning"

Not every dirty driveway just needs pressure washing. Here's when you might need actual repairs:

🚩 **Deep cracks** (wider than 1/4 inch) - Needs sealing or patching first
🚩 **Sunken sections** - Foundation/settling issue, not dirt
🚩 **Spalling** (flaking/peeling concrete) - Freeze/thaw damage, needs resurfacing

**Good news:** 90% of driveways just need a good clean. Pollen, mold, and tire marks make concrete look way worse than it actually is.

If you're unsure, I'm happy to take a look (no charge for a quick assessment). I'd rather tell you "just needs cleaning" than upsell you on something you don't need.

Text a photo to (910) 538-6552 if you want a second opinion.

## Posting Best Practices

✅ **DO:**
- Post 7-9 AM or 6-8 PM (when people are scrolling)
- Use local references ("in Holly Springs", "off Sunset Lake Rd")
- Respond to EVERY comment within 2 hours
- Use 1-2 emojis max (keeps it casual, not spammy)
- Include before/after photos when possible
- Tag your business page

❌ **DON'T:**
- Post more than 3x per week (gets annoying)
- Use all caps or excessive exclamation marks!!!
- Be pushy in comments
- Argue with negative comments (respond professionally, then DM)
- Post exact prices publicly (quote privately via text)
- Copy/paste the same post in multiple neighborhoods (they notice)

## NextDoor Etiquette

**When someone leaves a negative review:**
1. Don't get defensive publicly
2. Respond: "Sorry to hear that! Can you DM me so I can make this right?"
3. Resolve privately, then ask them to update review

**When competitors post:**
- Never badmouth them
- If someone asks for comparisons, say: "Can't speak for [other company], but here's what we offer..."

**When you see recommendation requests:**
- Jump in FAST (first comment wins)
- Keep it friendly, not salesy
- Offer free quote, not price
