# Homeowner Lead Strategy

## The Problem
Homeowners don't have business listings on Google Maps. We can't scrape residential emails like we can business emails.

## Legal & Effective Alternatives

### 1. **Neighborhood Targeted Mail** (What you're already doing)
- Door hangers in nice neighborhoods
- Focus on driveways that LOOK dirty (visual targeting)
- Spring pollen season = perfect timing

### 2. **NextDoor.com** ⭐ BEST FOR HOMEOWNERS
- Join neighborhood groups in your service area
- Post "spring cleaning special" offers
- Respond to "looking for recommendations" posts
- Build local reputation
- People ASK for quotes here (no cold outreach)

### 3. **Facebook Neighborhood Groups**
- Join local community pages (Holly Springs Neighbors, Apex Community, etc.)
- Post before/after photos
- "Member special" pricing
- Word-of-mouth spreads fast

### 4. **Thumbtack / HomeAdvisor / Angi** (Lead Generation Services)
- You pay per lead ($15-$30 each)
- They send you homeowners actively looking for pressure washing
- Pre-qualified, ready to buy
- Competes on price though

### 5. **Real Estate Agent Partnerships** ⭐ HIGH VALUE
- Agents need houses cleaned before listings
- Recurring business (every house they sell)
- Premium pricing (realtors don't haggle)
- Build 3-5 agent relationships = steady work

### 6. **HOA Contracts** ⭐ VOLUME DEALS
- One decision = 50-200 houses
- Annual contracts
- Less price sensitive (association budget)
- Slow to close but HUGE payoff

## Recommended Focus (Next 30 Days)

**Week 1-2:**
- Join NextDoor + all local Facebook groups
- Post 2-3 times per week (spring pollen, driveway transformations)
- Engage with "recommendation" posts

**Week 2-3:**
- Email blast to real estate agents (we have those templates)
- Offer "realtor special" pricing
- Goal: 3 agent partnerships

**Week 3-4:**
- Email blast to HOAs (we have those templates)
- Offer community discount pricing
- Goal: 1 HOA contract (even small = recurring work)

**Ongoing:**
- Door hangers in neighborhoods with visible dirty driveways
- Target after windy/pollen-heavy days

## Why Not Scrape Homeowner Emails?

1. **Legal risk** - Residential emails aren't public like business contacts
2. **Deliverability** - Personal emails mark bulk mail as spam faster
3. **Conversion** - Cold homeowner emails get <1% response vs 5-10% for local NextDoor posts
4. **Reputation** - One spam complaint tanks your email deliverability for ALL contacts

## The Winning Formula

**Homeowners:** NextDoor + Facebook + Door Hangers (local, visible, community-based)  
**B2B:** Email automation (property managers, HOAs, agents) ← What we're building now

Two different channels, two different strategies. Both work, don't mix them.
