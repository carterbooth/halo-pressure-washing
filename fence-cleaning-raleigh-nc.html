Wood, Vinyl & Chain Link Fences

Fences are one of the most visible features of your property, and they accumulate grime, algae, mildew, and weathering staining more than most homeowners realize. The right cleaning approach depends entirely on the material — and using the wrong technique can damage your fence permanently.

Wood Fences

Wood privacy fences in Raleigh are constantly exposed to moisture, shade, and humidity — ideal conditions for mold, mildew, and algae to thrive. Over time, wood turns gray, develops dark spots, and becomes slippery. We use calibrated pressure and appropriate cleaning solutions to safely remove biological growth and restore the wood's natural color without splintering or gouging the surface.

Vinyl Fences

Vinyl is popular for its durability, but it shows green algae and mildew staining clearly — especially in shaded areas. Vinyl can also yellow from UV exposure and oxidation. Our soft wash and targeted pressure cleaning removes all of this safely, restoring your vinyl fence to bright white or its original color without warping, cracking, or etching the surface.

Chain Link Fences

Chain link picks up rust staining, dirt, and organic matter in its galvanized coating and wire intersections. We clean chain link fences thoroughly using appropriate pressure and rust-treating solutions where needed. The result is a cleaner, brighter fence — particularly important for commercial properties, schools, and sports facilities where appearance matters.

How Fence Cleaning Extends the Life of Your Fence

Mold and mildew don't just stain your fence — they actively degrade the material over time. On wood, biological growth holds moisture against the wood fibers, accelerating rot. On vinyl, algae can work into micro-scratches and cause discoloration that becomes permanent if left long enough.

Regular cleaning — once every 1–2 years for most Raleigh homes — removes these organisms before they cause structural damage. A clean fence is also easier to inspect for loose posts, missing boards, or fastener corrosion that might need attention.

Think of fence cleaning as maintenance, not just aesthetics. A well-maintained fence lasts significantly longer and can avoid costly premature replacement. For a typical 6-foot privacy fence, that replacement can run $15–$30 per linear foot — far more than periodic cleaning costs.

Prep for Painting or Staining

Planning to repaint or restain your fence this season? A professional cleaning is the essential first step. Paint and stain applied over dirty, moldy, or poorly prepared wood will peel, blister, and fail within months — wasting your time and money on the finishing work.

Our fence cleaning service removes all existing mold, mildew, dirt, old peeling paint, and weathered gray wood fibers. We can also apply a wood brightener after cleaning to open the wood grain and neutralize cleaning solution residue, ensuring your stain or paint penetrates evenly and bonds properly.

Pro tip from Carter: For best results, allow your wood fence to dry for at least 48 hours after cleaning before applying any stain or sealant. This allows moisture to fully escape the wood grain and gives you the best possible adhesion.
