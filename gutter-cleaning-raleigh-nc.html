Why Clogged Gutters Are a Problem

Your gutters exist for one purpose: to move water off your roof and away from your foundation. When they're clogged with leaves, pine needles, and debris — a very common occurrence in Raleigh's tree-heavy neighborhoods — that water has nowhere to go. The consequences range from annoying to seriously expensive.

Roof Damage

Water pooling in clogged gutters can back up under roofing materials, causing rot and leaks that often go unnoticed until major damage has occurred.

Fascia & Soffit Rot

Overflowing gutters saturate the fascia boards they're attached to. Rotting fascia is expensive to replace and weakens the gutter attachment itself.

Foundation Issues

Water that can't drain properly flows down your home's exterior and pools around the foundation. Over time, this causes settling, cracking, and moisture intrusion in basements or crawlspaces.

Landscape Damage

Overflowing water cascades onto flower beds and landscaping, eroding soil and drowning plants. Concentrated overflow can also create trenches and pooling zones near your home.

Pest Infestations

Standing water and decomposing organic matter in gutters is a prime breeding ground for mosquitoes and an attractive nesting spot for birds, wasps, and squirrels.

Ice Dams in Winter

Clogged gutters freeze solid in cold weather, forming ice dams that force water back under shingles and into your home. Raleigh gets enough ice to make this a real risk.

Our Gutter Cleaning Process

1

Debris Removal

We hand-clear or blow out all debris from gutters — leaves, sticks, pine needles, shingle grit, and anything else that's accumulated. We bag and remove all debris from your property.

2

Flush & Flow Check

We flush the gutters with water to check flow, confirm downspouts are clear all the way through, and verify that water drains away from the foundation properly at each downspout exit.

3

Downspout Clearing

Clogged downspouts are often the real culprit. We use pressure and specialized tools to clear any blockages from top to bottom, ensuring full flow through the entire system.

4

Inspection Report

We flag any visible damage, sagging sections, or improper pitch that may be causing drainage problems. We'll let you know what we found so you can address it before it becomes a bigger issue.

Add-On: Gutter Brightening

Notice dark vertical streaks running down the outside face of your gutters? Those are called "tiger stripes" — a form of oxidation and staining that accumulates from roof runoff, tannic acid from leaves, and UV exposure. They don't respond to a simple rinse.

Our gutter brightening service uses a specialized chemical solution and gentle agitation to completely remove tiger stripes from aluminum and vinyl gutters. The result is gutters that look nearly new — bright white or their original color, without any staining.

How Often Should Raleigh Gutters Be Cleaned?

The Raleigh area presents a unique challenge for gutters: dense tree canopy, heavy pollen seasons, and significant leaf fall. Here's our recommended cleaning schedule based on your situation:

Standard Homes: Twice a Year

Most homes benefit from a spring cleaning (after pollen season, typically May) and a fall cleaning (after leaf drop, typically November or December). This keeps gutters clear through both the wet spring and winter months.

Heavy Tree Coverage: 3–4x Per Year

If you have pine trees, oaks, or other large trees directly over or near your roofline, gutters can clog within weeks. We recommend quarterly cleaning to stay ahead of the buildup and prevent any overflow events.

After Major Storms

Raleigh occasionally gets strong storms that load gutters with debris in a single event. After any significant storm, it's worth checking your gutters or scheduling a quick cleaning to ensure flow is restored.
