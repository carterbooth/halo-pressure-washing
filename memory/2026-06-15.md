### Lead Approval System (NEW):
**Workflow:**
1. Homeowner submits lead → Status: "pending" (invisible to contractors)
2. Heartbeat checks every 10 min for new pending leads
3. Sends Telegram/chat notification with lead details
4. Carter replies "approve" or "reject"
5. Approve → Lead becomes "available" to contractors
6. Reject → Lead deleted permanently

**API Endpoints:**
- `GET /api/leads/pending` - Get all pending leads
- `POST /api/leads/approve/:id` - Approve a pending lead
- `POST /api/leads/reject/:id` - Reject and delete a pending lead

**State Tracking:**
- `memory/lead-approval-state.json` tracks which leads have been notified
- Prevents duplicate notifications on subsequent heartbeats

**Commands:**
- "approve" or "approve [lead-id]" - Approves lead
- "reject" or "reject [lead-id]" - Rejects and deletes lead
