Professional Driveway Pressure Washing in Raleigh

Your driveway is one of the first things people see when they approach your home. A clean driveway signals a well-maintained property and significantly boosts curb appeal. If your concrete has turned gray with age, spotted with oil, or gone green with algae, Halo Pressure Washing can restore it.

We use professional-grade pressure washing equipment combined with hot water and specialized degreasers to tackle the toughest stains. Carter and the Halo team are based in Fuquay-Varina and serve Raleigh and the Triangle with fast, dependable service.

Beyond just looking better, regular driveway cleaning helps extend the life of your concrete. Algae and mold can work into surface pores, and freeze-thaw cycles can cause cracking if organic matter is present in those crevices. A clean driveway is a healthier driveway.

Common Driveway Problems We Fix

Every stain and surface issue has a solution.

Oil Stain Removal

Oil and grease stains from vehicles are among the most common and stubborn driveway problems. We apply professional degreasers and use hot-water pressure washing to lift and extract oil from concrete pores. While very old, deep-set stains may lighten rather than disappear entirely, fresh and medium-aged stains respond extremely well to our treatment. The result is a dramatically improved surface that looks clean and uniform.

Mold & Algae

Green, black, or slimy growth on your driveway is typically algae or mold — both thrive in Raleigh's humid climate, especially in shaded or north-facing areas. Beyond being unsightly, wet algae on concrete is a serious slip hazard. We eliminate it completely using pressure washing and biodegradable treatment agents that kill the growth at the source, keeping your driveway cleaner for longer between services.

Discoloration & General Staining

Over time, concrete driveways develop uneven discoloration from rust stains, fertilizer runoff, tree sap, and general weathering. Our high-pressure washing techniques restore a more uniform, clean appearance to the entire surface. We treat stained areas with appropriate cleaning agents before pressure washing to maximize stain removal and achieve the best possible results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does driveway cleaning cost in Raleigh, NC?

Driveway cleaning in Raleigh typically ranges from $100 to $300 depending on the size of the driveway and the level of staining. We provide free, no-obligation quotes — call (910) 538-6552 or request one online and we'll give you an accurate price for your specific situation.

Can pressure washing remove oil stains from concrete?

Yes, professional pressure washing with hot water and appropriate degreasers can significantly reduce or fully remove oil stains from concrete. The key is the combination of the right cleaning agents and professional equipment. Results vary depending on how long the stain has been set, but even older stains typically see dramatic improvement.

How often should I have my driveway pressure washed?

Most Raleigh homeowners benefit from driveway cleaning once every 1-2 years. Driveways with heavy tree coverage, significant vehicle traffic, or regular oil drips may benefit from annual cleaning. After your first cleaning, we can give you a personalized recommendation based on what we observe.

How long does a driveway cleaning take?

Most residential driveway cleanings take between 1 and 2 hours depending on the size and condition. We'll give you a more precise time estimate when we quote the job. The concrete will be damp after cleaning and should be given a few hours before driving on it.
