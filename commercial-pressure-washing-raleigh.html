Commercial Surfaces We Service

From storefronts to parking structures, we have the equipment and experience to handle large-scale commercial cleaning.

Parking Lots & Parking Decks

Oil stains, tire marks, gum, and general grime accumulate quickly in commercial parking areas. We use hot water and high-pressure surface cleaners to clean large concrete and asphalt areas efficiently. Regular parking lot cleaning protects the asphalt surface and improves the overall appearance of your property.

Building Exteriors

Commercial building exteriors — brick, EIFS, metal panels, concrete, glass curtain walls — accumulate algae, mold, and atmospheric staining over time. We clean commercial building exteriors safely using appropriate techniques for each material, restoring a professional appearance and removing contaminants that can cause long-term surface damage.

Loading Docks

Loading docks accumulate oil, grease, hydraulic fluid, and debris from constant truck and forklift traffic. These heavy-duty stains require industrial pressure washing with hot water and degreaser solutions. We work during off-hours to clean loading areas without disrupting operations.

Storefronts & Entrances

Your storefront is the face of your business. Gum on the sidewalk, algae on the facade, dirty windows and door frames — these all send the wrong message to customers. We clean commercial storefronts, sidewalk approaches, awnings, and entrance areas to keep your business looking sharp and inviting.

If you manage multiple properties — apartment complexes, commercial parks, HOA communities, retail centers — Halo Pressure Washing is equipped to serve your entire portfolio. Carter works directly with property managers and HOA boards to understand their properties, set maintenance schedules, and deliver consistent results across every location.
