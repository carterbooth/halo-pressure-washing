Concrete, Pavers, Flagstone — We Handle It All

Raleigh homeowners have a wide range of patio surfaces, each with its own cleaning requirements. At Halo Pressure Washing, Carter and our team know exactly how to treat each surface type for the best results without damage.

Concrete Patios

Concrete is porous and absorbs everything — oil, grease, algae, and water staining. It also develops efflorescence (white mineral deposits) over time. We use high-pressure surface cleaning with appropriate degreasers and cleaners to fully restore concrete patio surfaces, including removing stains that have penetrated the top layer.

Paver Patios

Paver patios are beautiful but require careful cleaning to preserve the jointing sand between pavers. Too much pressure in the wrong direction washes out the sand, destabilizing the whole surface. We use surface cleaners and targeted pressure techniques that clean the paver faces thoroughly while protecting the joints and base.

Flagstone & Natural Stone

Natural stone patios — flagstone, bluestone, limestone, travertine — need gentler handling than concrete. High pressure can pit or etch soft stone surfaces. We use lower pressure combined with stone-safe cleaning solutions to remove algae, lichen, and staining while preserving the natural texture and color of the stone.

Seasonal Patio Cleaning Tips

Raleigh's climate creates specific seasonal challenges for patio maintenance. Here's what we recommend throughout the year:

Spring (March–May)

The best time for an annual patio cleaning. Pollen season deposits a thick yellow coat on everything, and winter moisture accelerates algae growth. A spring cleaning sets your patio up for the whole outdoor entertaining season.

Summer (June–August)

High humidity and heat accelerate mold and algae growth, especially in shaded areas. If your patio gets afternoon shade, you may notice green spots appearing quickly. A mid-summer touch-up cleaning can keep it looking sharp all season.

Fall (September–November)

Falling leaves deposit tannins that stain concrete and pavers dark brown if left sitting wet. Cleaning in late October or November removes leaf staining before it becomes permanent and prepares the patio for winter.
