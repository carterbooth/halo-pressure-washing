Recurring Contracts Available

Property managers need predictability. An inconsistent cleaning schedule leads to visible deterioration between visits, reactive emergency calls, and tenants or residents who notice the property isn't being maintained. A recurring contract with Halo Pressure Washing solves all of that.

We work with property managers to create a custom maintenance schedule — monthly, quarterly, biannual, or custom — that keeps your properties consistently clean without requiring you to manage the calendar. We schedule in advance, confirm in advance, and show up as planned.

Monthly Service

Best for high-traffic commercial properties, restaurants, retail centers, or any property where curb appeal is business-critical. We clean common areas, walkways, dumpster pads, and building facades on a regular cycle.

Quarterly Service

Ideal for apartment communities, HOAs, and mixed-use properties. Four cleanings per year keeps the property looking sharp through all seasons and catches seasonal buildup before it becomes a problem.

Biannual Service

Spring and fall cleanings are the minimum effective schedule for most residential properties. We do a thorough job each visit, targeting the seasonal accumulation of pollen, algae, and leaf debris.

Multi-Unit & HOA Experience

Cleaning a single-family home and cleaning a 200-unit apartment community require very different approaches. Halo Pressure Washing has the equipment capacity and project management experience to handle large-scale multi-unit assignments efficiently.

For HOA communities, we commonly service:

Common Area Surfaces

Pool surrounds, clubhouse walkways, community sidewalks, playground equipment pads, and common parking areas. We clean these to HOA standards and document the work for your records.

Community Signage & Entrances

Monument signs, entrance gates, and community entrance hardscaping are the first thing residents and visitors see. We keep these focal points clean and presentable year-round.

Building Exterior Programs

For communities with shared building structures, we develop an exterior cleaning program that cycles through all buildings on a defined schedule, maintaining consistency across the whole community.

Fence & Perimeter Cleaning

Community fencing, retaining walls, and perimeter features accumulate algae and staining. We include these in comprehensive HOA cleaning programs to maintain the full property appearance.
