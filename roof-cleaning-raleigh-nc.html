Why Soft Wash — Not Pressure — for Roofs

Using a pressure washer on a roof is one of the most damaging things you can do to it. High-pressure water strips granules from asphalt shingles, voids manufacturer warranties, and can force water under tiles or into seams — leading to leaks and expensive repairs. Many roofing manufacturers explicitly state that high-pressure cleaning will void your warranty.

The correct method is soft washing: low-pressure application of biodegradable cleaning solutions that kill algae, moss, lichen, and mold at the root. The result is a thorough clean that lasts 2–5 years without causing any damage to your roofing material.

Black Streaks on Your Roof? Here's Why

Those dark streaks running down your shingles aren't just dirt — they're Gloeocapsa magma, a type of algae that feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles. Left untreated, it spreads across your entire roof, making your home look decades older than it is. But the visual impact is just the beginning.

Algae growth holds moisture against your shingles, accelerating deterioration and shortening your roof's lifespan. Moss and lichen do even more damage — their root systems physically break down shingle material over time. In Raleigh's humid, wooded environment, these organisms thrive. Most roofs in the Triangle area develop visible growth within 5–10 years of installation.

The good news: a professional soft wash treatment removes all of it and includes an algae inhibitor that slows regrowth significantly. It's far less expensive than a premature roof replacement.

Our Roof Cleaning Process

1

Inspection & Pre-Treatment

We walk the property, assess the roof type and condition, identify problem areas, and pre-wet surrounding landscaping to protect plants and grass during the cleaning process.

2

Soft Wash Application

We apply our professional-grade, biodegradable cleaning solution at low pressure to the entire roof surface. The solution penetrates and kills algae, mold, moss, and lichen at the cellular level.

3

Dwell Time

We allow the solution to dwell on the surface for the appropriate amount of time, letting the chemistry do the work without any abrasive scrubbing or high-pressure blasting.

4

Low-Pressure Rinse

After dwell time, we rinse the roof at low pressure, washing away all dead organic material. The result is a dramatically cleaner roof with no damage to shingles, tiles, or seams.

5

Final Walkthrough

We inspect the results, rinse down gutters and downspouts, clean up any overspray, and ensure you're completely satisfied before we leave.

Roof Types We Clean

Different roofing materials require slightly different approaches. We know each one.

Asphalt Shingle

The most common roof in Raleigh. Asphalt shingles are susceptible to Gloeocapsa magma algae (those black streaks) and moss. Our soft wash solution kills growth without stripping protective granules — keeping your roof warranty intact and extending its service life.

Metal Roofing

Metal roofs develop oxidation, algae, and mildew staining over time. We use appropriate cleaning solutions that address these issues without etching or damaging the finish. Metal roofs respond beautifully to soft wash treatment and look nearly new afterward.

Tile Roofing

Clay and concrete tile roofs in Raleigh accumulate moss and lichen that can crack tile if left unchecked. Our soft wash process removes all growth safely without disturbing the mortar, ridge caps, or tile placement. We take extra care around fragile tile surfaces.
